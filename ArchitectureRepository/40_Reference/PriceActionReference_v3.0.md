# PriceActionReference_v3.0

# Purpose

Reference definitions for price action concepts used throughout the
platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Trend

A sustained directional movement of price identified by higher
highs/higher lows (uptrend) or lower highs/lower lows (downtrend).

## Range

A market condition where price oscillates within defined support and
resistance.

## Breakout

Price exits a defined trading range with increased participation.

## Pullback

Temporary movement against the prevailing trend.

## Reversal

A confirmed change in market direction supported by structure and order
flow.

## Support

Price area where buying interest repeatedly appears.

## Resistance

Price area where selling interest repeatedly appears.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Usage Rules

-   Definitions are platform-wide SSOT.
-   Modules shall reference this document rather than redefine
    terminology.
-   Strategy documents may extend usage but not alter definitions.
