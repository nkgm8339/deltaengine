# MarketStatesReference_v3.0

# Purpose

Defines the canonical market state terminology used throughout the Order
Flow Analysis Platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Market States

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  State                               Definition
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Trending Market                     Market exhibiting sustained
                                      directional movement with
                                      structural confirmation.

  Ranging Market                      Market oscillating within
                                      established support and resistance
                                      boundaries.

  Balanced Market                     Auction is accepted around fair
                                      value with two-way participation.

  Imbalanced Market                   One side dominates, producing
                                      directional price discovery.

  Volatile Market                     Elevated price movement and
                                      expanded trading range.

  Quiet Market                        Reduced volatility and limited
                                      price movement.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Reference Rules

-   This document is the SSOT for market state terminology.
-   Architecture, Modules, Requirements, and Strategies shall reference
    these definitions.
-   Quantitative thresholds are implementation-specific and documented
    separately.
